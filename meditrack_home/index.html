Why MediTrack
My grandmother stared at three pages of English numbers she couldn't read — then waited four days to see the doctor.

Four days of silent worry, for a problem that had an answer. Much of health technology is still built for people who are already comfortable with medical language and English. I built MediTrack to make one part of healthcare feel less confusing for every Indian family — starting with the report itself.
