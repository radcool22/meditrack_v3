2What MediTrack does

MediTrack helps you understand your medical lab reports in plain language. You can:

  • Upload a lab report as a PDF, JPG or PNG — in the web app or by sending it on WhatsApp
  • Get a simple explanation of what each value means, which values are outside the normal range, and practical next steps
  • Ask questions by text or by voice — in the web app, on WhatsApp, or by calling MediTrack and talking to its AI voice assistant
  • Use MediTrack in Hindi, English, Marathi, Gujarati or Malayalam
  • Get a short Hindi video summary of a report
  • Keep your reports in one place and ask questions across all of them
  • Use basic health calculators (for example BMI and daily calories)

MediTrack is a health-literacy tool. It is not a medical service, a diagnostic tool, or a substitute for a doctor.

8Your data and privacy

What we collect

Your mobile number, your name (if you give it), your language preference, the reports you upload and the text read from them, MediTrack's explanations, your chat and WhatsApp messages, voice notes and their transcripts, phone call recordings, transcripts and summaries, and any videos made for you. If you request a video, you enter the patient's age; it is used only to check the video is appropriate and is not saved to your account.

How we use it

Only to provide MediTrack to you. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your medical data to train AI models.

How it is stored

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your reports are kept in private storage that only MediTrack's servers can read. Video summaries are stored on Cloudflare R2 and played through an unlisted link — anyone you share that link with can watch the video.

Deleting your data

When you delete a report in the app, the report file, its explanation, its chat history and its videos are deleted straight away. To delete your whole account and everything in it, contact us (see section 18) — we will do so within 30 days.
