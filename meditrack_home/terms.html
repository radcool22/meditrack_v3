Section 02

Description of Service

MediTrack is an AI-powered tool that allows users to:

  • Upload medical laboratory reports in PDF, JPG, or PNG format
  • Receive simplified plain-language explanations of report values
  • Interact with their reports via text and voice conversation in English and Hindi
  • Generate short Hindi video summaries of their reports
  • Access basic health calculators for BMI, daily calories, and VO2 max estimation
  • Track their medical report history over time

MediTrack is designed to help users understand their medical reports more easily. It is not a medical service, diagnostic tool, or substitute for professional medical advice.
